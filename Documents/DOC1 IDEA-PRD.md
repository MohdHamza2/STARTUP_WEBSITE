DOCUMENT 1 — IDEA → PRD
Startup Website & Lead Generation Platform
Document: Product Requirements Document — V1
Stage: Pre-development
Status: Draft → Ready for clarification
Primary objective: Build a modern startup website that attracts and converts two major customer audiences.
________________________________________
1. PRODUCT IDEA
Working concept
A modern digital company website with two primary business offerings:
A. Recruiting / Career Assistance
A platform/company that helps candidates, particularly international students and early-career professionals, with:
•	Finding relevant employment opportunities
•	Recruitment assistance
•	Job-search support
•	Application assistance
•	Career guidance
•	Reducing repetitive manual job-application work
•	Connecting qualified/interested candidates with the recruiting team
The website should make the visitor immediately understand:
"We help you navigate the US job market and make the process of finding and applying for opportunities easier."
The exact legal/business claim should be finalized before launch. We should not imply that we can guarantee employment or that we are an employer/recruiter for companies unless that is actually true.
________________________________________
B. Software & Digital Product Development
A second business line providing technology development services, including:
•	MVP development
•	SaaS development
•	End-to-end software products
•	Web applications
•	Business software
•	Websites
•	Portfolio websites
•	AI-powered applications
•	Automation systems
•	Industry-specific software
Potential industry focus:
•	Healthcare
•	Financial services
•	Agriculture
•	Industrial/business systems
•	Other domain-specific applications
The website should communicate this without overwhelming visitors with technical details.
________________________________________
2. PROBLEM
Problem — Candidate side
International students and recent graduates can struggle with:
•	Finding appropriate opportunities
•	Understanding which positions fit their profile
•	Repeatedly searching job boards
•	Repeatedly filling applications
•	Maintaining application pipelines
•	Following up
•	Understanding recruiting processes
•	Managing large numbers of applications
•	Knowing what recruiters actually look for
•	Getting lost among hundreds of applicants
The proposed service aims to reduce this friction.
Core candidate problem
Qualified candidates spend too much time manually searching, applying and managing their job search instead of focusing on their career, studies and preparation.
________________________________________
Problem — Business/software side
Organizations, entrepreneurs and individuals may have:
•	An idea but no engineering team
•	A need for an MVP
•	An existing manual process that should be automated
•	A requirement for a website
•	A need for an internal business application
•	An idea for a SaaS product
•	An industry-specific software requirement
Core software problem
People and organizations often have technology ideas or operational problems but lack the technical resources to turn them into reliable software products.

26. VIDEO
Video can be used for:
•	hero background
•	product demonstrations
•	abstract brand visuals
•	software development visuals
•	recruiting workflow visualization
But:
Video should not automatically play a huge full-resolution video above the fold.
21st.dev's own recent guidance notes that autoplay hero video can compete with JavaScript during the critical first second and that CSS/canvas-based effects can sometimes achieve the visual effect at lower cost. 21st
Therefore:
Video is optional and should be used only where it materially improves the experience.
